# Lecture 37.07 · The Double-Submit Problem: Reload After a POST

> **Section:** S37 · **Type:** 📖 Theory · **Track:** ★ Core · **Time:** ~10 minutes

---

## 1. The scenario

A servlet that answers a successful POST **directly** with a page (a forward, or HTML):

```text
POST /tasks/new  title=Pay invoice   →  200  <html>Task created!…   (the URL bar: /tasks/new)
```

The user presses **Reload**. The browser repeats the last request, the **POST**, after a "Confirm Form Resubmission" dialog most people click through. Result: two "Pay invoice" tasks. The same happens with Back then Forward, or an impatient double-click on a slow page.

For TaskFlow, a duplicate title is refused (`UNIQUE`), but for comments, payments or emails there's no natural key: every repeat is a new row, a new charge, a new email.

## 2. Two separate problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Reload/Back repeats the POST** | never answer a successful POST with a page: **redirect** to a GET (PRG, 37.08) |
| **double-click** / two quick POSTs | disable the button on submit (UX), plus server-side idempotency where it matters (a unique key, or a one-time token per form) |

## ✅ Recap

If the response to a successful POST is a page, Reload repeats the POST. PRG makes the page the user sees the result of a **GET**, so Reload is harmless.
