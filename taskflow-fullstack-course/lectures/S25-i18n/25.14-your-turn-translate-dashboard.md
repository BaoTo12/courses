## Requirements

1. A new namespace **`dashboard`**: `public/locales/en/dashboard.json` and `public/locales/vi/dashboard.json`. It's loaded only when the dashboard is opened.
2. Translate `DashboardPage` and the four widgets in `DashboardWidgets.tsx`: headings, "Loading…", "Nothing overdue. 🎉".
3. The completion widget says "**75% of 1 task done**" / "**75% of 12 tasks done**", with correct plurals in both languages.
4. The overdue widget shows a sentence above the list: "**You have 1 overdue task**" / "**You have 5 overdue tasks**" / "Bạn có 5 công việc quá hạn".
5. Each overdue task shows a **localised** due date with a relative part: "due Sep 20, 2026 (6 days ago)" / "hạn … (6 ngày trước)". Use `formatDue` (25.10) with the **current** language.
6. Status names come from `common` (`status.*`), not copied into `dashboard.json`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Two namespaces in one component</summary>

`useTranslation(['dashboard', 'common'])`, then `t('byStatus')` and `t(\`common:status.${status}\`)`.
</details>

<details><summary>Plural + another value</summary>

`t('completion.summary', { count: total, rate })`. `count` selects the form, and `rate` is only interpolated. Vietnamese needs only `_other`.
</details>

## Check

- Switch the language on the dashboard: everything changes, including dates.
- Add these to `src/i18n/i18n.test.ts`: `t('dashboard:overdue.summary', { count: 5 })` in English, and `{ count: 1 }` in Vietnamese.
