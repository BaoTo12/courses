The course brief's JSTL exercise ("empty list → message; non-empty → table; some rows get a different label; zebra rows with `varStatus`"), applied to the comments of a task. Start from 35.14's provided comment store and servlet.

## Requirements

**Model and controller:**
1. A view model `CommentDetails` (the `Comment` + its author `User`, which may be `null`).
2. `TaskDetails` gains `List<CommentDetails> getComments()`, filled by `TaskViewServlet`, oldest first.

**View (`view.jsp`), a `<section id="comments">`:**
1. A heading `Comments (N)`, with N from `fn:length`.
2. No comments → `<p class="text-muted">No comments yet.</p>`, and **no** table in the HTML.
3. Otherwise a `<table class="comments">` with one row per comment:
   - `<td class="row-number">#1</td>`, `#2`, … from `varStatus`;
   - the author's display name, `Deleted user` if the author is `null`, followed by a `<span class="badge">Admin</span>` for admins (`User.isAdmin()`);
   - the body, **escaped**;
   - the creation time.
4. Rows alternate `class="comment--odd"` (1st, 3rd, …) and `class="comment--even"`.
5. The add-comment form under the list (from 35.14).
6. Also convert the rest of `view.jsp` to `<c:out>`. Use its `default` for "No category" and "no due date".

**Tests to pass (write them first):** task 4 → `Comments (0)` and `No comments yet.`; task 1 → `Comments (2)`, an odd and an even row, `#2`, an author cell `Admin <span class="badge">Admin</span>`, an author cell `Alice Nguyen`; a posted `<img src=x onerror=…>` comment is rendered as text.

## Hints

- `varStatus="row"` → `${row.count}`, `${row.count % 2 == 0 ? … : …}` (35.07).
- `<c:out value="${item.author.displayName}" default="Deleted user"/>`: the default is used when **any** step of the chain is `null`.
- If a space you wrote in the page is missing from the output, read 35.22.
