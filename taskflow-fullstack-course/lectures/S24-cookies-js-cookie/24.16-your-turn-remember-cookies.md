## Requirements

Add two cookies through `cookies.ts` only (24.05), in `src/features/preferences/rememberedCookies.ts`.

**A. Language: `tf_lang`**

- `rememberLanguage(language: 'en' | 'vi')` and `readRememberedLanguage(): 'en' | 'vi' | undefined`.
- Remembered for **one year**, for this **browser**, regardless of who logs in.
- S25's language switcher calls `rememberLanguage`, and i18next's detector reads the same cookie. Nothing in the UI uses it yet.

**B. Last visited task: `tf_last_task`**

- Write it when a task's details page has **loaded** a task (not for an invalid or missing id).
- A **session cookie**: gone when the browser session ends.
- **Removed on logout and on session expiry.** It points at the user's own data.
- On the Tasks page, if the cookie names a task in the cache, show "Continue where you left off: *title*" as a link.

**Both:** validate what you read. Edit the cookies in DevTools to `fr` and `1;alert(1)`, and both readers must return `undefined`.

## Questions to answer in your solution

1. Which attributes does each cookie get, and why? (Lifetime, path, `SameSite`, `Secure`, `HttpOnly`.)
2. Why does the language survive logout, but the last task doesn't?
3. Could the "last task" feature leak anything to another user of the same computer? What limits it?

## Tests

In a jsdom test, write each cookie, edit it to garbage, and check that the reader rejects it. `src/api/cookies.test.ts` is a model.
