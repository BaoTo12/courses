## Part A: build it (scriptlets only, on purpose)

1. A task is **overdue** when it has a due date **before today** and isn't `DONE`.
2. Above the table, if any task is overdue: `3 overdue tasks` (or `1 overdue task`), in `text-danger`.
3. Each overdue row gets `class="task-row--overdue"`, and a ⚠ after its date.
4. No changes to the servlet (yes, that's the point of the exercise).

**Test it:** create a task with due date `2020-01-01` through the form, then assert the list contains `task-row--overdue`, `2020-01-01 ⚠`, and the count sentence.
