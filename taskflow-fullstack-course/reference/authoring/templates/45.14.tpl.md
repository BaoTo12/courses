- ✍️ `web.xml`: `<async-supported>true</async-supported>` in **every** `<filter>`. Without it, `startAsync()` fails with "A filter or servlet of the current chain does not support asynchronous operations".
- ✍️ `AppContextListener`: `Executors.newFixedThreadPool(2)` at startup, `shutdownNow()` at shutdown (45.08).
- **🛡 CSV injection:** a spreadsheet treats a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` as a **formula**; a task titled `=HYPERLINK(…)` would become a live link, or worse, in the admin's Excel. Prefixing `'` makes it text. Quoting every text cell and doubling `"` handles commas, quotes and line breaks.
- The owner scope applies: `service.find(TaskFilter.all(), user)` exports your tasks, or all of them for an admin (42.09).
- A link for users: `<a href="<c:url value='/tasks/export.csv'/>">Export CSV</a>` on the list page.
