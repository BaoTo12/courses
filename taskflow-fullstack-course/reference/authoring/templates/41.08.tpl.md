| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **POST**, with the CSRF token | a logout is a state change: as a GET link, any page could log your users out (`<img src="/taskflow/logout">`) |
| `invalidate()`, not `removeAttribute("currentUser")` | everything tied to the login goes: the CSRF token, the history, any flash. Nothing survives into the next person's use of that browser |
| the flash after `invalidate()` | `Flash.put` calls `getSession()`, which creates a **new**, anonymous session to carry "You have been logged out." |
| audit `LOGOUT` | who was logged in, until when |

The nav shows a Log out button (a small form) whenever `currentUser` is set (41.19).

## ✅ Checkpoint

Log out: you land on the login page with the message, and `/taskflow/tasks` redirects to login again.
