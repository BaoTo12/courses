## 1. What goes in the session

✍️ `src/main/java/com/taskflow/security/AuthUser.java`

{{file:@reference/taskflow/backend/src/main/java/com/taskflow/security/AuthUser.java:java}}

Id, username, display name, role: enough to show the header and decide permissions. Not the hash, not the email, not the whole row (38.09).

## 2. The check: a service

✍️ `src/main/java/com/taskflow/service/AuthService.java`

{{file:@reference/taskflow/backend/src/main/java/com/taskflow/service/AuthService.java:java}}

📦 Provided behind it: `UserDao.findCredentials(username)` (the only query that reads `password_hash`) and `AuditDao.record(type, username, ip, details)` (INSERT into the append-only `audit_events`, 36.10). `AppContextListener` builds `AuthService` with a `PasswordHasher` and a `LoginThrottle(5, 20, 15 minutes)`.

## 3. The controller

✍️ `src/main/java/com/taskflow/web/auth/LoginServlet.java`

{{file:@reference/taskflow/backend/src/main/java/com/taskflow/web/auth/LoginServlet.java:java}}

On success, in this order:
1. `request.changeSessionId()`: the pre-login id (which an attacker may know) stops working (41.11);
2. store the `AuthUser` under `currentUser`;
3. `Csrf.reset`: the logged-in session gets a new token;
4. a flash "Welcome, …" and a 303 to the checked `returnUrl` (41.17).

## 4. The page

✍️ `src/main/webapp/WEB-INF/views/auth/login.jsp`

{{file:@reference/taskflow/backend/src/main/webapp/WEB-INF/views/auth/login.jsp:jsp}}

The typed username comes back; the password field is always empty. `autocomplete="username"` / `"current-password"` let password managers do their job.

## 5. Who created the task? Now we know

`TaskCreateServlet` sets `task.setOwnerId(CurrentUser.get(request).getId())`, and comments get the logged-in author: 37.11's "from the session, never from the form", completed.
